Apollo II — Cosmic "WIRE" visualizer attribution
================================================

The WIRE immersive scene is an original Jetpack Compose Canvas reimplementation
inspired by the look and triangle-band displacement approach of:

  https://github.com/nixonyh/UnityAudioVisualizer
  Copyright contributors to nixonyh/UnityAudioVisualizer
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0

Apollo does NOT embed Unity, VFX Graph assets, Burst jobs, or that repository's
shader/mesh binaries. Only the aesthetic concept (wireframe sphere + spectrum-
driven triangle displacement + radial energy) was recreated for Android.
