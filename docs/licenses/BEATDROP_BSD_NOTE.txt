Apollo II — BeatDrop / MilkDrop inspiration note
================================================

https://github.com/mvsoft74/BeatDrop is a MilkDrop2-style desktop visualizer
(BSD-3-Clause wrapper around MilkDrop heritage code).

Apollo does NOT embed BeatDrop, MilkDrop, DirectX, or ns-eel presets.
BEAT_TUNNEL / BEAT_CASCADE are original Jetpack Compose Canvas scenes inspired
by soft beat-reactive warp / cascade aesthetics only.
