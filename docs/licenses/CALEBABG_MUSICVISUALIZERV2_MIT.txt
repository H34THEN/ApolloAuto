Apollo II — MusicVisualizerV2-inspired visualizer attribution
=============================================================

Realtime Compose scenes BASS_NATION, BUBBLE_BASS, CIRCULAR_PULSE,
COLOR_SIGNAL, DUAL_SIGNAL, MIXED_WAVE, SHAPE_WAVE, and STAR_FIELD are
inspired by visualization concepts from:

  https://github.com/CalebABG/MusicVisualizerV2
  Copyright (c) 2020 Caleb B. Gardner
  MIT License

Apollo does NOT embed Processing, Minim, ControlP5, or the desktop JAR
player. Styles were reimplemented for Jetpack Compose Canvas using
Apollo's existing playback-session Visualizer FFT/waveform feeds.
